# MCA Journey: install it as an app on your phone

This folder is a complete installable web app (a PWA). It has no server and no database.
Your data is saved on your own phone.

## Step 1: Put it online (free, about 10 minutes)

### Option A: Netlify Drop (easiest, no account setup needed beyond sign-up)
1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign up for free.
2. Drag this whole `mca-app` folder onto the page.
3. Netlify gives you a link such as `https://something.netlify.app`.

### Option B: GitHub Pages (best for your resume)
1. Create a free GitHub account and a new repository, for example `mca-journey`.
2. Upload all the files in this folder (index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js and the three icons).
3. Open the repository Settings, then Pages. Choose the main branch and the root folder, then Save.
4. After a minute your site is live at `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/mca-journey/`.

Use the repository link and the live link on your resume.

## Step 2: Install it on your phone

- **Android (Chrome):** open your link, tap the three-dot menu, then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
- **iPhone (Safari):** open your link, tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen".

It opens full screen with its own icon and works offline after the first visit.

## Good to know

- **Your data is saved on that phone only.** Use Settings > Download backup now and then. To move to a new phone or a different browser, open Settings > Restore and choose the backup file.
- Opening the app in a different browser, or clearing the browser data, starts you with an empty app.
- If you edit the files later and upload them again, close and reopen the app once to see the update.
- The website is public, but your data never leaves your phone. The files contain no personal information.
- Reminders: installed web apps cannot reliably ring at a set time. Use your phone's Clock app for a morning alarm.
